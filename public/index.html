Your library stays yours

Private by design, not by promise.

afterglow has no accounts and no backend database. Your export is read and kept in your own browser, and you can delete it whenever you like.

  • Nothing is uploaded

    Your history files are processed on this device.

  • No accounts, no tracking

    No Spotify login, no analytics, no third-party fonts or scripts.

  • Covers by name only

    When real artwork is on, only names and track IDs are looked up. Never what or when you played.

Good things take a little history

Get your listening history.

  1. 01
    Request your Spotify data

    On Spotify’s account privacy page, ask for Extended streaming history. It covers your whole account, skips and shuffle included.

    Open Spotify privacy settings
  2. 02
    Wait for the email

    Spotify prepares the export and emails you a download link. It can take a few days.

  3. 03
    Drop it in here

    Import the ZIP as it is, or the Streaming_History_Audio_*.json files inside. The shorter account data export (StreamingHistory*.json) works too.

No Spotify login, no uploads, no tracking. Your history is read on this device.
